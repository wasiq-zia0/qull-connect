# Terms of Service — Unclaimed Property

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

_Note: these terms are pending final review by counsel and are not final legal terms._

## What this service does

Unclaimed Property searches all 51 US state unclaimed-property portals for your name (and prior names), builds per-state claim packs, and tracks recoveries.
Everything happens in a chat conversation; there is no app to install.

## What this service does NOT do

- **We file claims with state treasuries on your behalf.**
- **We store your Social Security number (never asked for or stored).**
- **We guarantee funds exist.**
- We do not store or view your payment card details — card handling is done by Stripe.

## Fee

- **10% of recovered funds, at most — lower if your state caps it.**
- You are told, in plain language, **before** you save a card:
  > "You will be charged 10% of recovered funds — charged once, after you confirm."
- You pay 10% of the unclaimed funds you actually recover (or your state's lower
  legal maximum — California, Indiana and Nebraska cap finder fees at 10%) —
  nothing if the search finds nothing.
- The charge happens **once per recovery**, only after you confirm. It is charged
  off-session to the card you saved. Duplicate charges are prevented: an
  already-billed recovery can never be charged again; a recovery in a different
  state is billed separately.

## Refund policy

- No fee unless you confirm a recovery. Erroneous charges refunded in full within 30 days.
- To request a refund, contact support (below).

## Support

- Email: support@qull.io
- We aim to respond within 2 business days.

## Privacy

- We store your full legal name, prior names, email, and states of residence — plus date of birth only with your explicit consent.
- Payment data is processed by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy; we keep only
  customer IDs and charge references.
- We never sell your personal information.
