Plain-language terms. Last updated: 2026-09-19.

_Note: these terms are pending final review by counsel and are not final legal terms._

## What this service does
Unclaimed Property Finder helps you locate money or assets a U.S. state may be
holding in your name (uncashed checks, dormant accounts, refunds, and similar
unclaimed property). It:
- searches no private databases — it points you to each state's official
  unclaimed-property portal and the national NAUPA-endorsed site MissingMoney.com;
- builds per-state claim packs (portal link, step-by-step filing instructions,
  document checklist, pre-filled cover sheet);
- tracks your per-state claim status.

## What it does NOT do
- It never files claims for you and never submits your personal information to
  any state or third party. You file every claim yourself.
- It does not guarantee that unclaimed property exists in your name, or that a
  filed claim will be approved. States decide claims on their own criteria.
- It does not collect your Social Security number, ever.

## Fees — exact terms
- You pay **10% of the recovered unclaimed funds, at most** (lower if your
  state's law caps finder fees — California, Indiana and Nebraska cap at 10%),
  and only if **you confirm the recovery**.
- You are told the exact fee in plain language **before** you save a card:
  "You will be charged 10% of the recovered unclaimed funds, only if you
  confirm the recovery. No charge otherwise."
- No charge is made at signup, at card save, or for claim packs. Nothing is
  charged until you tell the service a recovery was paid out, and you confirm
  the amount. Each recovery in a different state is billed separately; an
  already-billed recovery is never charged twice.
- Payment is processed by Stripe (Meta's payments partner) as an off-session
  charge against the card you saved.

## Cancellation and refunds
- You can cancel at any time before a charge is made — simply don't confirm a
  recovery, and nothing will ever be billed.
- If a fee was charged and you believe it was in error (for example, the
  recovery amount was wrong or the state later reclaimed the funds), contact
  support within 30 days for a review and refund of the fee.

## Your data
- We store only what is needed to build claim packs and track status: your
  name, prior names, email, states of residence, and (only with your explicit
  consent) your date of birth — some states ask for it when you file.
- We never collect or store your SSN.
- Your data is used only to provide this service. It is not sold, shared with
  marketers, or sent to any state. You may ask for your data to be deleted at
  any time.
