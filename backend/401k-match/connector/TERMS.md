# Terms of Service — 401(k) Match Finder

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

_Note: these terms are pending final review by counsel and are not final legal terms._

## 1. What this service does
401(k) Match Finder is a financial-education tool. Given the salary, pay
frequency, current 401(k) contribution rate, and employer match formula you
provide, it computes how much employer match you are currently capturing,
how much is going uncaptured, and a recommended contribution percentage and
step-by-step plan to capture the full match.

## 2. What it does NOT do
- It is **not financial advice**, investment advice, or tax advice.
- It does not access your 401(k) account, move money, or change contributions for you.
- It does not guarantee any outcome — actual match depends on your plan's
  formula, vesting schedule, contribution timing, and IRS limits.
- **Always confirm your match formula and contribution rules with your plan
  administrator before making changes.**

## 3. Fee terms
- The service costs a **flat $99.00 per year**, billed once annually.
- You will be charged a flat $99.00 per year for the match analysis and fix

  then is the $99.00 charged off-session.

- Payments are processed by Stripe; card details are never stored on our servers.
