# Terms of Service — Bill Negotiator

## What this service does

Bill Negotiator analyzes your bill, generates provider-specific negotiation scripts with talking points and escalation paths, and calculates your documented savings.
Everything happens in a chat conversation; there is no app to install.

## What this service does NOT do

- **We call or negotiate with your provider on your behalf.**
- **We guarantee a rate reduction.**
- **We provide legal advice.**
- We do not store or view your payment card details — card handling is done by Stripe.

## Fee

- **35% of your documented savings.**
- You are told, in plain language, **before** you save a card:
  > "You will be charged 35% of your documented savings — charged once, after you confirm."
- You pay 35% of the annualized savings you confirm after a successful negotiation — nothing if the negotiation fails.
- The charge happens **once**, only after you confirm. It is charged off-session to the card
  you saved. Duplicate charges are prevented: an already-billed case can never be charged again.

## Refund policy

- No fee is charged unless you confirm savings. Erroneous charges are refunded in full within 30 days.
- To request a refund, contact support (below).

## Support

- Email: support@qull.io
- We aim to respond within 2 business days.

## Privacy

- We store your provider name, bill amounts, and account details you share to build the script.
- Payment data is processed by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy; we keep only
  customer IDs and charge references.
- We never sell your personal information.
