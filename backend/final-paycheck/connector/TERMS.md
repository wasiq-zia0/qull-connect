# Terms of Service — Final Paycheck Recovery

_Last updated: 2026-09-19_

## What this service does

Final Paycheck Recovery helps employees recover unpaid final wages after a
job separation. It:

1. Records your case details (employer, dates, wages owed, state).
2. Computes the payment deadline from your state's wage-payment law.
3. Generates a demand-letter PDF citing your state's statute — only after the
   legal deadline has passed — for **you** to send to your former employer.
4. After you confirm wages were recovered, charges a contingency fee via Stripe.

## What this service does NOT do

- It does **not** provide legal advice, and it does not create an
  attorney-client relationship. Demand letters are generated by automated
  templates. Consult a licensed employment attorney in your state before
  acting.
- It does **not** send letters, emails, or any communication to your employer
  or anyone else. You send the demand letter yourself.
- It does **not** file wage claims or lawsuits on your behalf.
- It does **not** guarantee any recovery. If you recover nothing, you pay nothing.

## Fee terms (exact)

- **Fee:** 25% of the wages you confirm were recovered.
- **Trigger:** charged only after **you** confirm a recovery amount
  (`/recovery-confirmed`). Confirmation is entirely in your control.
- **How:** charged off-session to the card you saved during billing setup.
- **No recovery, no fee.** If your case never recovers wages, nothing is ever charged.
- The fee is charged at most **once per case**.

### Example

You confirm $2,000 in recovered wages → you are charged **$500** (25% of $2,000).

## Cancellation and refunds

- You can cancel at any time **before** a charge: simply do not confirm a
  recovery, or ask support to close your case. Closing your case means no
  charge can be made.
- **Refunds:** once a fee is charged after your confirmed recovery, it is
  non-refundable — the service was performed. If you believe a charge was
  made in error, contact support within 30 days and it will be investigated.

## Your data

Your case details are stored in the connector's database to track deadlines
and billing. They are never sold or shared with third parties except:

- Stripe, for payment processing (subject to Stripe's terms and privacy policy);
- as required by law.
