# Terms of Service — Moving Concierge

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

_Note: these terms are pending final review by counsel and are not final legal terms._

## What this service does

Moving Concierge builds you a personalized, state-specific address-change pack when you move:
a step-by-step checklist covering USPS mail forwarding, your new state's driver's-license /
ID deadline, voter registration, banks and credit cards, employer/payroll, insurance,
utilities, subscriptions, IRS notification, and more — plus a printable PDF.
Everything happens in a chat conversation; there is no app to install.

## What this service does NOT do

- **We never file an address change on your behalf.** Every change-of-address is completed
  by you, through the official links we provide (usps.com, your state's DMV, vote.gov, etc.).
- We do not provide legal, tax, or financial advice. Links and deadlines are informational;
  always confirm requirements with the official agency.
- We do not store or view your payment card details — card handling is done by Stripe.

## Fee

- **Flat $49.00 per move.** No contingency, no meter, no upsells.
- You are told, in plain language, **before** you save a card:
  > "You will be charged a flat $49.00 for the complete address-change pack. Charged once, after you confirm."
- The charge happens **once**, only after you confirm. It is charged off-session to the card
  you saved. Duplicate charges are prevented by idempotency: an already-paid move can never
  be charged again.

## Refund policy

- **Full refund** if your pack was never generated or delivered (e.g. payment succeeded but
  pack generation failed, or you cancel before the pack is unlocked).
- **After the pack is generated:** refund requests are handled fairly and at our discretion —
  partial or pro-rated refunds where appropriate, and full refunds for genuine mistakes
  (e.g. duplicate moves, wrong state entered at intake) within 30 days of the charge.
- To request a refund, contact support (below).
