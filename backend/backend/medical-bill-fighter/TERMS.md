# Terms of Service — Medical Bill Fighter

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

_Note: these terms are pending final review by counsel and are not final legal terms._

## What this service does

Medical Bill Fighter audits line items for common billing errors, generates dispute letters, itemized-bill requests, financial-assistance applications, and negotiation scripts.
Everything happens in a chat conversation; there is no app to install.

## What this service does NOT do

- **We contact your provider or insurer on your behalf.**
- **We provide medical or legal advice.**
- **We guarantee a reduction.**
- We do not store or view your payment card details — card handling is done by Stripe.

## Fee

- **25% of your documented reduction.**
- You are told, in plain language, **before** you save a card:
  > "You will be charged 25% of your documented reduction — charged once, after you confirm."
- You pay 25% of the amount your bill is reduced by (errors found, disputes won, negotiated discounts) — nothing if the bill doesn't budge.
- The charge happens **once**, only after you confirm. It is charged off-session to the card
  you saved. Duplicate charges are prevented: an already-billed case can never be charged again.

## Refund policy

- No fee unless you confirm a reduction. Erroneous charges refunded in full within 30 days.
- To request a refund, contact support (below).

## Support

## Privacy

- We store your bill details and line items — health-adjacent data handled with extra care and never sold or shared.
- Payment data is processed by Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy; we keep only
  customer IDs and charge references.
- We never sell your personal information.
