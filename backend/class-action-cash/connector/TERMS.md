# Class Action Cash — Terms of Service (plain language)

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

## What this service does
Class Action Cash watches open class-action settlements and matches them
against your email receipts. When it finds a likely match, it prepares a
claim pack: an eligibility checklist, step-by-step filing instructions, and a
pre-filled information sheet. **You file the claim yourself** on the
settlement's official website. The service never files claims for you, never
sends email, and never auto-files anything.

## What it doesn't do
- It is not a law firm and does not give legal advice. Eligibility is your
  call; every claim is filed under penalty of perjury.
- It does not guarantee you are eligible, that your claim will be approved,
  or that any settlement will pay out.
- It never sends, replies to, forwards, or deletes your email. Gmail access
  is strictly read-only.

## The fee — stated plainly before you ever save a card
**You will be charged 20% of the confirmed settlement payout, only if you
confirm the payout. No charge otherwise.** The exact fee is shown in the app
and spoken by the agent before you save a card. There is no fee for scanning,
matching, or generating claim packs — those are free.

Example: if a settlement pays you $100 and you confirm it, you are charged
$20. If you never confirm a payout, you are charged $0.

## How the fee is collected
When you're ready, you save a card once through a secure Stripe setup. The
card is charged off-session only after you personally confirm that a
settlement payout arrived. You will see the amount and the 20% fee before any
charge.

## Cancellation and refunds
- **Cancel any time:** you can delete your saved card / billing setup at any
  time before a charge, and nothing will be charged.
- **No charge without your confirmation:** a fee is charged only after you
  tell the agent "payout confirmed" with the amount.
- **Refunds:** if you were charged a fee in error (for example, a payout you
  didn't actually receive), contact support within 30 days and we will
  investigate and refund the fee.

## Your responsibilities
File only claims you genuinely believe you are eligible for. Filing a claim
you are not entitled to is a false statement on a court-supervised form, and
settlement administrators reject and may refer fraudulent claims.

## Privacy
Receipt matches and billing records are stored in the connector's own
database to run the service. They are not sold or shared with third parties.
Stripe processes card data under Stripe's own terms; the connector never
stores full card numbers.
