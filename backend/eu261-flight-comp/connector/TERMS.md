# Terms of Service — EU261 Flight Comp

_Last updated: 2026-09-19_

## What this service does

EU261 Flight Comp is a template-automation tool. You tell it about a delayed,
cancelled, or overbooked flight; it checks the facts against Regulation (EC)
No 261/2004 and, where the rules appear to apply, generates a draft claim
letter you can send to the airline yourself.

## What it does NOT do

- It does **not** file claims with airlines, courts, or enforcement bodies. **You** file.
- It is **not legal advice** and not a substitute for a lawyer. Every claim
  pack is labeled as template automation, not legal advice.
- It does not verify airline delay causes, booking records, or ticket details.
  The verdict is only as good as the facts you provide.
- Extraordinary-circumstances disputes (weather, strikes, security) are
  fact-specific and often contested — get legal advice if the airline pushes back.

- You are charged **only if you confirm the airline paid you**, and only after

- **No charge otherwise.** If the airline pays nothing, you pay nothing.
- Before you save a card, we show you this exact deal in plain language:

## Cancellation and refunds

- You can **cancel at any time before confirming a payout** — just don't
  confirm, and nothing is ever charged.
- If a fee was charged in error (wrong amount, duplicate charge), contact
  support and we will reverse it. Refunds are issued to the original card.
